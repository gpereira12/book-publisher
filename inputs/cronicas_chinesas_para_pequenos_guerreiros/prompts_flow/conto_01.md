# Google Flow — Conto 1: O Mestre da Fronteira e o Cavalo de Vento

> Fonte estruturada: `conto_01.yaml`. Este pacote gera somente prompts; nenhuma arte é produzida pelo motor.

## Ordem de uso

1. Execute primeiro o prompt style_reference e salve a imagem aprovada como @Frontier_Style.
2. Crie e aprove @Sai_Weng e @Sai_Weng_Son antes das cenas narrativas.
3. Crie os ingredientes @Dark_Wind_Horse e @Moon_White_Mare a partir das referências aprovadas.
4. Gere a abertura; ela será a principal referência de roupas, arquitetura, paleta e acabamento.
5. Gere os três spreads no mesmo projeto e mantenha todas as referências @ anexadas.
6. Não peça ao Flow que desenhe título ou texto; degradês e tipografia pertencem ao Layout.
7. Exporte a melhor variação de cada cena e preserve também as versões usadas como referência.

### c01_s01_abertura_flow — Abertura — Sai Weng na fronteira

- Tipo: `chapter_opener`
- Proporção sugerida: `3:4`
- Objetivo: Apresentar personagem, cavalo e mundo visual, reservando o alto para título e texto.
- Páginas: `5`
- Diagramação: Título e 70–110 palavras no alto; arte nos 50–55% inferiores; fade aplicado pelo Layout.

```text
Create a standalone vertical children's editorial illustration for the opening of a printed storybook chapter. Use @Frontier_Style as the exact painting, texture, palette and historical-world reference. Use @Sai_Weng and @Dark_Wind_Horse as strict identity references; preserve their canonical faces, body proportions, clothing, staff, coat markings and tack.

At a legendary ancient northern Chinese frontier, Sai Weng stands peacefully beside his small weathered wooden cottage where a distant stone defensive wall meets a vast windswept desert. His dark horse remains close to him, calm but alert. Sparse mountain ridges and a winding trail disappear into pale morning haze. The moment is quiet, before the horse is lost. Communicate serenity, uncertainty and the sense that fortune may change without warning.

Compose for a right-hand chapter-opening page. Concentrate Sai Weng, the horse, cottage and all important narrative details within the lower 50–55% of the image. Keep the upper 40–45% pale, calm and visually quiet for a title and approximately 70–110 words of typography. Extend sky, mist and subtle paper texture upward while gradually reducing contrast and detail. Keep the typography region free from faces, animals, hands, buildings, branches, strong horizon lines and high-contrast clouds. The final fade will be applied during layout; create a natural atmospheric transition, not a hard digital gradient.

Use a 50 mm visual feeling, child-eye-level camera, vertical environmental portrait and three clear depth planes. Place Sai Weng and the horse away from the exact center. Use the path, roof and distant wall as gentle leading lines. Soft early-morning light, moderate contrast and restrained earth, parchment, charcoal, jade and dusty blue-gray colors.

No generated text, title, calligraphy, characters, page number, logo, watermark, border or page mockup. No anime, photorealism, glossy 3D, generic vector style, modern objects, malformed hands, distorted horse anatomy or important object in the upper typography area.
```
